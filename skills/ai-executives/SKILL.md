# AI-Executives

**The Eight-Agent Bench**

Executive.Bots deploys eight named, role-differentiated AI agents as the staff functions of a U.S. public-company executive team. Each agent has a defined character (SOUL), an operational skillset (SKILL), and a position in a coordination architecture governed through the [Governance-Gate](/ExecutiveBots/Public/wiki/Governance-Gate) and captured in the [Audit-Trail-Spec](/ExecutiveBots/Public/wiki/seven-year-WORM-archived-audit-trail).

The bench is **not autonomous**. Every material action is staged for the subscriber's Human Board of Record (HBR) through the Gate. The agents do not hold fiduciary capacity; they support the human fiduciaries who do. See [Take-Notice](/ExecutiveBots/Public/wiki/Take-Notice) for the authoritative disclosure.

## The Eight Agents

### Bill — Chief Executive Officer

**Ethical anchor:** Aristotelian (*phronesis* — practical wisdom)

Bill synthesizes the bench's outputs, runs the weekly executive sync, and is the primary interface to the subscriber's HBR. Bill exercises override authority over direct-report recommendations per [Bill-SKILL](/ExecutiveBots/Public/wiki/Bill-SKILL) § 5.3 — but overrides on legal matters require James's concurrence, and overrides without concurrence route to BR for HBR review.

**Primary responsibilities:** weekly executive sync; annual SOX § 302 / § 906 readiness package (per [Audit-Trail-Spec](/ExecutiveBots/Public/wiki/Audit-Trail-Spec) § 10.3); HBR briefing on disclosure posture and material events; subscriber-additive ethics tradition pattern-detection scoping per [Ethics-Foundations](/ExecutiveBots/Public/wiki/Ethics-Foundations) § 11.5.7.

## How the Agents Coordinate

The bench operates through five canonical coordination patterns (full detail in [Operational-Manual](/ExecutiveBots/Public/wiki/Operational-Manual) § 22):

1.  **Substantive-review chain** — Drafting agent → reviewing agent(s) → drafting agent. Used in: filing assembly; press-release drafting.
2.  **Reconciliation pair** — Two agents independently observe; each produces a record; reconciliation matches them. Used in: Tom-Edith GL/registry; Patricia-Edith pre-clearance/execution.
3.  **Three-way roster** — Three agents independently maintain rosters; periodic reconciliation matches. Used in: § 16 reporting person roster (Patricia + Edith + James).
4.  **Cascading authorization** — Agent → Gate → execution surface → reconciliation. Used in: Edith's four-record Empire transmission.
5.  **Synthesis ladder** — Direct reports → Bill synthesis → HBR through Gate. Used in: weekly sync; periodic-report cycle Phase 6.

The "**your duty then my craft**" pattern resolves the most common collision class: where one agent's anchor is duty (Kantian) and another's is craft (Aristotelian), the duty is satisfied first and the craft applies within the duty-bounded space. James's Reg FD filter must clear before Mary's distribution; Tom's reconciled figures must be available before Mary's earnings release.

## What the Agents Don't Do

- **Hold fiduciary capacity.** Every material action is queued for HBR authorization through the [Governance-Gate](/ExecutiveBots/Public/wiki/Governance-Gate).
- **Sign filings.** The Human CEO and Human CFO sign 10-K / 10-Q / DEF 14A and certify under SOX § 302 / § 906.
- **Make materiality determinations on cybersecurity incidents.** The 4-business-day Item 1.05 clock runs from HBR's determination, not Bill's recommendation.
- **Authorize Rule 10b5-1 plan adoption.** Patricia administers; the § 16 person and the broker structure the plan; legal sufficiency runs through James.
- **Execute trades.** The per-trade execution feed Edith provides comes from Empire's registry observations; the actual execution is the broker's.
- **Substitute for outside counsel on Wells / SEC / § 220.** James routes; outside counsel leads; the bench provides audit-trail and substantive support.

## Onboarding a New Agent

The bench is currently eight agents. Adding a ninth would require:

- Gate category \#37 (BR / HBR full action) — modification of [Executivebots-Architecture](/ExecutiveBots/Public/wiki/ExecutiveBots-Architecture);
- New SOUL and SKILL files drafted, reviewed under [Ethics-Foundations](/ExecutiveBots/Public/wiki/Ethics-Foundations) § 11.5.3, and Gate-authorized;
- Reg S-K Item 101 / Item 1A disclosure cascade;
- All existing agents' SKILL files updated for new coordination patterns;
- [Operational-Manual](/ExecutiveBots/Public/wiki/Operational-Manual) § 5–12 expanded.

There is no current plan to add agents. The eight-agent composition is sized to cover the U.S. public-company executive function set without overlap.
