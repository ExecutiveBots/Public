## → Subscriber's Human Board of Record (HBR)

**Why you are here:** you are a director of a subscriber issuer. You retain all fiduciary capacity. The bench's eight agents stage actions for you to authorize through the [Governance-Gate](/ExecutiveBots/Public/wiki/Governance-Gate). You sign Form 10-K. You certify under SOX § 302 / § 906. The audit trail backs you up.

**What you must understand:**

### What You Cannot Delegate

The single-principal structure makes the following non-delegable to the bench:

- **Fiduciary judgment.** Every Gate authorization is yours alone. There is no Board of independent directors to backstop you.
- **Recusal substitution.** When the matter implicates you personally, outside counsel's attestation is the structural substitute — but outside counsel does not authorize, you do.
- **DGCL § 144(a)(3) fairness path.** The only available related-party safe harbor for transactions with you or entities you control. The bench documents the fairness analysis; you (with outside counsel input) make the determination.
- **§ 302 / § 906 certifications.** You sign in every executive capacity (CEO + CFO + principal accounting officer if applicable) per Form 10-K General Instruction D.(2).
