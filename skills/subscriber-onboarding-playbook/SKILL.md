## §0 Purpose & Sequence Overview

### 0.1 Why Deterministic Onboarding

A subscriber onboarded by an ad-hoc process is a subscriber whose audit trail begins with an unrepeatable configuration event. Every downstream *Caremark* defense, every Wells submission's "what was the company's compliance posture as of date X" inquiry, every SOX § 302 certification of disclosure controls — all of these rest on an inspectable, reproducible deployment baseline. The deterministic sequence in this document supplies the baseline.

The defense surface this enables is documented in [Defense-Posture](/ExecutiveBots/Public/wiki/Defense-Posture) § Caremark / *Marchand* — *Marchand v. Barnhill*, 212 A.3d 805 (Del. 2019), held that the absence of a board-level monitoring system for a mission-critical compliance area was *Caremark*-actionable; this playbook produces the demonstrable monitoring system.

### 0.2 The 30-Day Sequence
