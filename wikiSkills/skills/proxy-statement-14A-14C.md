# Proxy Statements (14A) and Information Statements (14C)

**ExecutiveBots Executive Team · Domain Reference** *annual meeting · written-consent path · disclosure*

## 0. Purpose, Scope, Triggers

### 0.1 What this skill is

Reference for the annual proxy (Schedule 14A) or information statement (Schedule 14C). Most subscribers will file 14A annually for the annual meeting; single-principal subscribers (where a controlling shareholder acts by written consent) typically file 14C instead.

### 0.2 Triggers

- Annual meeting calendar approaching (typically 90–120 days before meeting).
- Written-consent action by controlling shareholder requiring 14C distribution.
- Special meeting (charter or bylaw amendment, merger, sale of assets, going-private).
- Rule 14a-8 shareholder proposal submitted.
- Director nomination process initiation under advance-notice bylaw.

## 3. Filing Mechanics

### 3.1 Preliminary vs. definitive

| Filing                | When required                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
|-----------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Preliminary (PRE 14A) | 10 calendar days before definitive sent to security holders, **unless** the proxy relates only to (i) election of directors, (ii) auditor ratification, (iii) say-on-pay or say-on-frequency, or (iv) approval / adoption of certain compensation plans. Most uncontested annual proxies skip preliminary. |
| Definitive (DEF 14A)  | When first sent to security holders                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |

If a single non-exempt matter is on the proxy (e.g., charter amendment), the full proxy must be filed preliminary — including the routine elections and auditor ratification.

### 3.2 Notice and access (Rule 14a-16)

| Method            | Mechanic                                                                                                                         |
|-------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Full set delivery | Mail full proxy + annual report to all security holders                                                                          |
| Notice and access | Mail "Notice of Internet Availability" 40 calendar days before the meeting date; provide Internet copy and paper copy on request |
| Hybrid            | Notice and access for some holders, full set for others                                                                          |

### 3.3 Annual report mailing

Pursuant to Rule 14a-3, the annual report to security holders must accompany or precede the proxy for the annual meeting at which directors are elected. The annual report may be the Form 10-K itself or a separate annual-report wrap.

### 3.4 EDGAR

DEF 14A submission type. Distribute on EDGAR same day as mailed. The annual report is also submitted on EDGAR (ARS or 10-K with wrap).

## 4. Item 8 — Executive Compensation (Cross-Reference)

Schedule 14A Item 8 incorporates Reg S-K Item 402 in its entirety. *See* `item-402-executive-compensation-SKILL.md` for the full disclosure framework including:

- Summary Compensation Table (Item 402(c))
- Grants of Plan-Based Awards (Item 402(d))
- Outstanding Equity Awards at Fiscal Year-End (Item 402(f))
- Option Exercises and Stock Vested (Item 402(g))
- Pension Benefits (Item 402(h))
- Nonqualified Deferred Compensation (Item 402(i))
- Compensation Committee Report (Item 407(e))
- Compensation Discussion and Analysis (CD&A) (Item 402(b))
- CEO Pay Ratio (Item 402(u))
- Pay-versus-Performance (Item 402(v)) SRC and EGC accommodations:
- SRC and EGC: scaled Item 402 — no CD&A; reduced tables; no PvP.
- EGC during EGC period: also no pay ratio.

## 5. Item 6 — Security Ownership

Disclosure of beneficial ownership of more than 5% of any class of voting securities, and of all directors and executive officers as a group. Includes:

- Name and address of each 5% holder.
- Amount and nature of beneficial ownership.
- Percentage of class. Footnote convertible securities, options, warrants exercisable within 60 days (Rule 13d-3(d)). Group attribution under §13(d).

Cross-reference to Schedule 13D / 13G filings is best practice.

## 6. Item 7 — Directors and Executive Officers; Item 407 Governance

### 6.1 Item 401 disclosure

For each director and executive officer:

- Name, age, position, term.
- Business experience for past 5 years.
- Other directorships (past 5 years).
- Family relationships.
- Involvement in certain legal proceedings (Item 401(f) — bankruptcies, criminal proceedings, civil actions, SRO disciplinary actions).

### 6.2 Item 407 disclosure

| Sub-item | Subject                                                       |
|----------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| 407(a)   | Director independence                                         |
| 407(b)   | Board meetings and committees                                 |
| 407(c)   | Nominating procedures                                         |
| 407(d)   | Audit committee — composition, financial expert, report       |
| 407(e)   | Compensation committee — composition, report                  |
| 407(f)   | Stockholder communications                                    |
| 407(h)   | Board leadership structure and board's role in risk oversight |
| 407(i)   | Hedging policies (employees, officers, directors)             |

Director independence determinations follow the applicable listing standard (NYSE Section 303A.02; Nasdaq Rule 5605(a)(2)). The proxy specifies which directors are independent and identifies the standard applied.

## 7. Item 9 — Auditor; Item 14 — Audit Committee Report

### 7.1 Item 9 (Independent Registered Public Accounting Firm)

- Identify the auditor.
- Disclose aggregate fees by category (audit; audit-related; tax; all other) for the two most recent FYs.
- Pre-approval policies (SOX §202).

### 7.2 Item 407(d)(3) — Audit Committee Report

Required content:

1.  Whether the audit committee has reviewed and discussed audited FS with management.
2.  Whether the audit committee has discussed with the auditor matters required by PCAOB AS 1301 and Rule 2-07 of Reg S-X.
3.  Whether the audit committee has received the auditor's written disclosures regarding independence per Independence Standards Board Standard No. 1 (now PCAOB Rule 3526) and has discussed independence with auditor.
4.  Whether, based on the above, the audit committee has recommended inclusion of audited FS in the 10-K. Report signed by all audit committee members.

*See* `audit-committee-operation-SKILL.md`.

## 8. Item 404 — Related-Person Transactions

### 8.1 Disclosure trigger

Any transaction since the beginning of the last FY (or proposed) in which:

- Amount involved exceeds \$120,000 (SRC: lesser of \$120,000 or 1% of average total assets); **and**
- A related person had or will have a direct or indirect material interest.

### 8.2 Related persons

- Directors and director nominees.
- Executive officers.
- 5% beneficial owners.
- Immediate family members of the above.

### 8.3 Required disclosure

- Name of related person and relationship.
- Material terms.
- Amount of the transaction.
- Amount of related person's interest.
- Other material information.

### 8.4 Approval policy disclosure (Item 404(b))

Description of policies and procedures for review, approval, or ratification of related-person transactions; standards applied; persons authorized; transactions not subject to the policy.

### 8.5 Common misses

- Compensation to immediate family member of director — must be disclosed if exceeds \$120,000.
- Indemnification advances — not exempt; must be disclosed.
- Brand-name services from a related-person-owned entity — disclose even if "arm's length pricing."
- Continued use of director-owned property post-separation — disclose if amount exceeds threshold.

## 9. Item 406 — Code of Ethics

### 9.1 Disclosure

- Whether the issuer has adopted a code of ethics applicable to the CEO, CFO, PAO, and persons performing similar functions.
- If not, explain why. If adopted, the code must be filed as Exhibit 14 to the 10-K **or** posted on the issuer's website **or** undertaken to be provided free of charge upon request.

### 9.2 Waiver disclosure

Material amendment to, or waiver of, any provision of the code granted to PEO, PFO, PAO, or controller must be disclosed within 4 business days on:

- Form 8-K Item 5.05; **or**
- Issuer's website (with prior notice in last 10-K).

## 10. Item 405 — Late §16 Filings

Disclosure of any §16 insider who filed a Form 4 late during the fiscal year, by name and number of late filings, based on review of Forms 3/4/5 furnished to the issuer.

Disclosure standard: based on a "review of the \[§16 filings\] furnished to the issuer and written representations from reporting persons." Issuer may rely on representations that no Form 5 was required.

## 11. Item 24 — Say-on-Pay; Item 408 — Trading Arrangements

### 11.1 Item 24 say-on-pay

Pursuant to Dodd-Frank §951:

- **Say-on-pay** advisory vote on executive compensation — at least once every three years.
- **Say-on-frequency** vote on whether say-on-pay should be every 1, 2, or 3 years — at least every six years. Result is non-binding (advisory). Compensation committee must consider the result.

SRC say-on-pay applicability and frequency follow the standard rules; EGC during EGC period is exempt.

### 11.2 Item 408 trading arrangements

Issuer-level disclosure on insider-trading policy (Item 408(a)(2)) and timing of stock option / SAR grants in relation to MNPI (Item 402(x)). Cross-reference to quarterly 10-Q disclosures on §16 insider 10b5-1 plan adoption / modification / termination. *See* `section-16-and-rule-10b5-1-SKILL.md`.

## 12. Rule 14a-8 — Shareholder Proposals

### 12.1 Eligibility

Holder must (as of submission date):

- Own at least \$2,000 in market value of voting securities for at least 3 years; or \$15,000 for 2 years; or \$25,000 for 1 year.
- Hold continuously through the meeting date.

### 12.2 Procedural requirements

| Requirement                              | Detail                                                                                   |
|------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Proposal length                          | 500 words or less                                                                        |
| Number of proposals                      | One per holder per meeting                                                               |
| Submission deadline                      | At least 120 calendar days before the anniversary of the prior year's proxy mailing date |
| Holder statement of intent to be present | Required                                                                                 |

### 12.3 Exclusion bases (Rule 14a-8(i))

13 enumerated bases for excluding a proposal:

1.  Improper under state law.
2.  Violation of law.
3.  Violation of proxy rules.
4.  Personal grievance / special interest.
5.  Relevance (less than 5% of assets, sales, net earnings).
6.  Absence of power / authority.
7.  Management functions (ordinary business).
8.  Director elections.
9.  Conflicts with company's own proposal.
10. Substantially implemented.
11. Duplication.
12. Resubmission (3-year cooldown if vote thresholds not met).
13. Specific amount of dividends.

### 12.4 No-action process

To exclude, issuer files no-action request with SEC Division of Corporation Finance at least 80 calendar days before filing definitive proxy. Staff issues no-action letter or denies. James + outside counsel coordinate.

## 13. Advance-Notice Bylaws

Subscriber's bylaws typically require shareholders nominating directors or proposing business outside Rule 14a-8 (i.e., to be presented from the floor, not in the proxy) to provide advance notice — typically 90–120 days before the anniversary date of the prior year's meeting.

Compliance with the advance-notice bylaw is a condition of the floor presentation. Deficient notice may be rejected by the chair. Litigation risk if rejection is challenged.

## 14. Schedule 14C — Information Statement

### 14.1 When 14C applies

Schedule 14C is used when corporate action is taken by **written consent** of shareholders rather than at a meeting. No proxy is solicited because the action is already taken (or will be taken upon majority written consent). The information statement is sent to non-consenting shareholders.

Typical 14C scenarios for single-principal subscribers:

- Controlling shareholder consents to charter amendment.
- Controlling shareholder consents to reverse stock split.
- Controlling shareholder consents to name change or reincorporation.
- Controlling shareholder consents to compensation plan adoption.
- Controlling shareholder consents to election of directors (some states permit).

### 14.2 Twenty-day rule

Schedule 14C information statement must be **mailed at least 20 calendar days before** the action is to be taken. If the action requires shareholder approval but the controlling shareholder has already consented, the 20-day period runs **after** consent and **before** effectiveness of the action.

For Delaware corporations, DGCL §228(e) requires prompt notice (often within 10 days) of action by written consent to non-consenting shareholders. The 20-day Schedule 14C window typically supersedes the DGCL minimum.

### 14.3 14C content

Schedule 14C Item set parallels Schedule 14A — Items 1 through 24 are largely the same. Differences:

- No proxy card.
- No revocability discussion.
- Statement that no vote is required from the recipient.

### 14.4 Preliminary 14C

Schedule 14C must be filed in preliminary form (PRE 14C) at least 10 calendar days before the definitive is mailed, unless certain exemptions apply. The exemptions parallel Schedule 14A.

## 15. Schedule 14F-1 — Change in Majority of Directors

Separate from 14A/14C. Triggered by acquisition of securities resulting in a change of majority of directors otherwise than at a meeting (e.g., reverse-merger closing). Filed and mailed at least **10 calendar days** before the change. Common in shell-company reverse mergers.
